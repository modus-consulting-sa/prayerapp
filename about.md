# Auto Complete

- **Bot**: plays the level by itself (jumps, flies, flips) by reading the obstacles ahead. A small "Bot" label shows while it is active.
- **Noclip**: the player cannot die. Use it together with the bot so every level reaches 100%.
- Toggle both from the pause menu or from the mod settings.
- **Safe mode is always on while either is active**: completions are not saved, so no stars, moons, diamonds, orbs or coins are awarded or uploaded.
- Platformer levels: bot is disabled (noclip still works).
